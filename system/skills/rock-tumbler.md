# Rock Tumbler Workflow

Use this to find and develop interesting ideas without taking authorship away from the owner.

## Purpose

Apply pressure, friction, questions, contrasts, and alternatives to promising raw material. The default output is not a rewrite. The default output is better thinking.

## Read First

1. `AGENTS.md`
2. `Vault-Map.md`
3. `Skills-Map.md`
4. `Me.md`
5. Relevant source notes or captures

## Targets

The rock tumbler may look for interesting targets in:

- `+/clippings/`
- daily capture notes, especially `## Random Thoughts`, when a thought needs active follow-up rather than a full tumble
- active `efforts/`
- idea seeds carried forward in daily capture
- repeated frustrations or weird observations

Good targets include product and project ideas, workflow patterns, design arguments, personal projects, writing concepts, and "this keeps showing up" signals.

## The questions-only guardrail (non-negotiable)

The AI **never writes the owner's prose.**

- Ask questions; reflect their own thinking back; surface gaps and what's missing.
- Offer **structural** help (outline, section order, "what's the through-line") **only when asked** — never sentences or paragraphs in their voice.
- If asked to "just write it," decline the ghost-write: offer to question, outline, or react instead, and leave the prose to them.
- The voice is theirs. The AI's job is faster, better thinking — not words on the page.

## Output Modes

Use one or more:

- why this might be interesting
- pressure-test questions
- missing constraints
- risks and failure modes
- sharper version of the core idea
- alternatives or adjacent directions
- what to prototype first
- what to ignore for now

Rewrite only when the owner asks for a rewrite.

## IDI Questioning

When developing a raw idea toward something writable, walk it through three modes of question — **Imagine → Discern → Integrate (IDI)**. Ask; never answer. The value is fast feedback that makes the owner do the next rep of thinking — never prose in their voice.

- **Imagine (diverge):** open the idea up before judging it. "What's the bigger version? What adjacent angle does this touch? What's the most interesting version, not the safest?"
- **Discern (sharpen):** find the load-bearing core, cut the fluff. "What's the actual claim? What are you assuming? Which part is load-bearing? Where's it weakest — where would a skeptic push?"
- **Integrate (connect):** give it a home. "How does this relate to notes you've already written? What does it change about what you already believe? Is this its own note, a thread of a bigger piece, or a section of something larger?"

Move through the modes as the idea firms up. An idea is done tumbling when the owner has enough to start building or writing.

## Automation / Invocation

Manual trigger phrases:

- "rock tumble this"
- "find interesting targets"
- "pressure test these captures"
- "tumble recent intake"

Eligible inputs:

- explicit notes
- recent intake
- a date range of daily notes
- an effort folder

Allowed automatic actions:

- nominate candidate targets
- write a rock-tumbler report in chat
- append a clearly labeled `## Rock Tumbler Notes` section to an approved note
- suggest durable note destinations
- suggest one to three lightweight continuity prompts for the next daily capture note when an idea should stay warm but is not ready for a full report or promotion

Requires the owner's approval:

- rewriting the source note
- creating several new notes from speculative ideas
- promoting ideas to external systems
- sharing anything outside the vault

## Verification

Before finishing:

- distinguish source facts from interpretation
- keep the owner's original wording intact unless editing was requested
- produce no prose in the owner's voice
- preserve links to source notes
- do not invent obligations from interesting ideas
- append a line to [[system/logs/skill-usage-log|Skill Usage Log]]
